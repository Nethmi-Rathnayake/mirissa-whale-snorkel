# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Travelers planning a trip to Sri Lanka, mostly first-time visitors to Mirissa, researching and booking a whale/dolphin tour from a phone or laptop, often days or weeks in advance. Many have never snorkeled with whales before and are weighing safety, ethics, and price across several competing operators. There is no login or dashboard; every visitor is a prospective guest evaluating whether to trust this operator with an unusual, once-in-a-trip experience.

## Product Purpose

Convert a marketing visit into a booking inquiry (a `mailto:` contact, no in-app checkout). The site exists to build enough trust and desire that a stranger emails to book a specific tour package. Success looks like a visitor understanding what's included, feeling reassured about safety/ethics, and reaching a package's booking link with no confusion about price or logistics.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, premium. Boutique eco-tour operator, not a mass-market excursion reseller — reassuring rather than adrenaline-driven, confident rather than salesy. Should feel like a small, expert, caring crew, not a template travel agency.

## Anti-references

Generic template-y travel booking sites: interchangeable stock-photo card grids, default Bootstrap-agency layouts, anything that reads as mass-produced rather than specific to Mirissa and this crew.

## Design Principles

1. Specificity over stock-template genericness — every section should read as belonging to this operator and this place, not a swappable travel template.
2. Trust before upsell — safety, experience, and what's included should be legible before price pressure.
3. Warm premium restraint — quality communicated through typography, spacing, and photography, not badges, banners, or urgency tactics.
4. Calm confidence — the tone reassures a first-timer rather than hyping adrenaline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: sufficient color contrast against the ivory/cream palette, visible focus states on all links/buttons, and no motion-only affordances (existing `motion-reduce` handling in `Reveal` should be preserved/extended).
